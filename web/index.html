What this is

Stage one of the port: the asset pipeline and renderer. The tile sheet, the cellmap and the palette come out of LEVELnn.PH exactly as load_level reads them, and the scroll clamp uses the original's own (tiles − viewport) × 16 arithmetic.

There is no player and no physics yet. The tile blitter at 0x00436B2C has been read, so the cell word's draw-mode bits are confirmed — bit 12 picks the blit path, bits 13–14 select variants, and bit 15 is never tested while drawing. What bit 15 does mean is still open: on ceil3 it covers exactly the walls and floor, on forest1 it misses obvious platforms. Toggle solid cells and flag nibbles to see the data. Collision, the sprite record format and the movement constants all remain.
